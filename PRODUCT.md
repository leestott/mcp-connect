# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and operators exploring a fictional pharmaceutical recall workflow, including human approval and audit evidence.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate MCP tool contracts, deterministic authority over consequential actions, and observable recall decisions with synthetic data. The local browser demo must remain usable without a model or cloud account.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, operational, and trustworthy. The interface should make control state and evidence easy to inspect, not dramatize them.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon or high-gloss dashboard effects that distract from the existing Control Tower workflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep the operator's task and the consequences of each action clear.
- Distinguish verified control evidence from agent recommendations.
- Preserve familiar controls and consistent state language across local and hosted modes.
- Make the interface legible in different ambient lighting without changing the meaning of status colors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
